01 — ABOUT THE PROJECT

Editing shouldn't take hours.

Our project aims to reduce the repetitive work involved in turning long-form videos into engaging short-form content.

🎬 What are we building?

VidioAI is an AI-assisted video editing platform designed to analyze a long video, identify valuable moments and prepare short-form content automatically.

⏱️
Manual editing takes too much time.
🔍
Finding the best moments is difficult.
📝
Captions and formatting require extra work.

💡 Our Goal

Create a simple system where a user can upload a video and let intelligent algorithms assist with the repetitive parts of editing.


The project combines web development, artificial intelligence and multimedia processing into one platform.
